It's a pattern, not one moment

Coercive control is a pattern of behaviour that slowly takes away someone's freedom. Each act can look small on its own.

Watching and checking

Going through your phone, tracking where you are, or needing to know who you talk to.

Money and isolation

Controlling your money, stopping you from working, or cutting you off from friends and family.

Writing it down helps
